# Beyond the Firmament Contributor Agreement

**Draft v0.1 — for attorney review before public launch**

This agreement is intended to make collaboration possible while protecting both contributors and the shared universe of **Beyond the Firmament**.

## 1. You own what you create

You retain copyright in your original contribution unless you separately agree otherwise in writing.

Submitting work does not transfer ownership of your full story, artwork, game, character design, or other original work to Beyond the Firmament.

## 2. Rights granted when you submit material

By submitting material for possible inclusion in the project, you grant Beyond the Firmament a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to review, display, discuss, archive, edit for formatting and consistency, and reproduce the submitted material as reasonably necessary to operate the project and evaluate it for publication or canon.

## 3. Additional rights for accepted canon

If your contribution is expressly accepted into official canon, you grant Beyond the Firmament a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to:

- reproduce and publish the accepted canonical material;
- edit or adapt it for continuity, formatting, reference works, timelines, maps, summaries, databases, and other canon materials;
- incorporate its accepted ideas, events, locations, institutions, technologies, characters, terminology, and other canonical elements into the shared universe;
- allow other approved contributors to refer to and use those canonical elements in official Beyond the Firmament works;
- use those canonical elements in commercial Beyond the Firmament products, including books, games, visual media, merchandise, reference works, and adaptations.

You continue to own your original expression and complete work, subject to the rights granted above.

## 4. Complete stories and other standalone works

Acceptance of an idea, character, event, or other element into canon does **not** automatically give Beyond the Firmament the right to commercially republish your complete standalone story, novel, screenplay, artwork, game, or other finished work outside the normal operation of the project.

Commercial publication of a complete contributor work may require a separate publishing or licensing agreement unless the contributor expressly agrees otherwise.

## 5. Shared canon

Once an element is accepted into official canon, it becomes part of the shared fictional universe.

Other approved contributors may create official works that refer to, develop, or interact with that canonical element, subject to editorial review.

Acceptance into canon does not give any contributor exclusive control over the accepted canonical element.

## 6. Editorial authority

The Canon Editor has final authority over what becomes official canon and may accept, reject, revise, or remove material from canon for continuity, quality, legal, editorial, scientific, or project-direction reasons.

Submission does not guarantee publication or acceptance into canon.

## 7. Attribution

Beyond the Firmament will make reasonable efforts to credit contributors for substantial accepted contributions where practical.

Minor edits, general ideas, factual corrections, brainstorming, and collaborative discussion may not always receive individual credit.

## 8. Contributor promises

By submitting material, you represent that:

- you have the right to submit it;
- it does not knowingly infringe another person's copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, or other rights;
- it is not copied from another fictional universe or protected work without permission;
- you will disclose meaningful use of third-party licensed material when submitting it.

## 9. Withdrawal

Before material is accepted into canon, you may withdraw it by written notice.

After material has been accepted into canon, the rights already granted to Beyond the Firmament remain in effect so that the shared universe, existing publications, and other contributors' works are not broken by later withdrawal.

You may still use and develop your own original work, subject to the shared-canon rights granted here.

## 10. Commercial projects and compensation

Acceptance into canon does not by itself create a right to payment, royalties, ownership in Beyond the Firmament, or participation in revenue.

If Beyond the Firmament wishes to commercially publish or commission a contributor's complete standalone work, compensation and additional rights may be addressed in a separate written agreement.

## 11. No partnership or employment

Contributing to the project does not create an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, agency relationship, or ownership interest in Beyond the Firmament.

## 12. Changes to this agreement

Future submissions may be governed by updated versions of this agreement. Material already accepted into canon remains governed by the agreement in effect when it was accepted unless the contributor and Beyond the Firmament agree otherwise in writing.

## 13. Acceptance

A contributor should not submit material for official canon unless they have read and agreed to the current Contributor Agreement.

The project should establish a clear GitHub-based method for recording acceptance before public submissions begin.

---

**Important:** This is a working project draft, not legal advice. It should be reviewed by an attorney familiar with copyright, publishing, licensing, and collaborative creative projects before Beyond the Firmament begins accepting public contributions.
