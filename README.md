# Beyond the Firmament

**Beyond the Firmament** is a collaborative fictional universe about a small human community escaping a despotic Earth and building a self-sustaining civilization among the stars.

Around the year 2100, governments have used artificial intelligence, autonomous weapons, pervasive surveillance, behavioral manipulation, and other technologies to concentrate power to an unprecedented degree. A clandestine group concludes that reform may no longer be enough.

They leave.

Their vessel, the **Peregrine**, begins as a heavily modified cargo aircraft carrying 160 founders, a biological ark, fission and fusion systems, high-energy propulsion, radiation shielding, and an experimental self-building structural skeleton.

The Peregrine can repair itself, acquire matter, and gradually construct new structure around itself. Over generations it grows from an escape craft into a worldship with neighborhoods, farms, forests, weather, animals, and its own culture.

The founders and their descendants call themselves **the Remnants**.

Their first great objective is to escape Earth, cross beyond Mars, use the Solar System to build velocity and mass, survive the Sun Dive and outer-system passage, and eventually reach Alpha Centauri.

But the Peregrine is not merely transportation. It is meant to become a permanent home.

## What contributors can create

Contributors are invited to:

- write short stories, novellas, scripts, and other fiction
- propose additions to the canon
- explore science, engineering, ecology, and ship design
- develop cultures, governments, religions, and institutions
- examine philosophical questions raised by the setting
- create games and simulations
- create visual art, maps, diagrams, and other media

## Canon structure

### Core Canon
The foundation of the universe: major events, technologies, physical rules, and facts that other work should respect.

### Expanded Canon
Community-created material that has been reviewed and accepted into the shared universe.

### Non-Canon Works
Stories, games, artwork, alternate histories, and experiments that use the setting without becoming official history.

## How canon changes

Discussion creates ideas. **Pull requests propose canon. Merging makes it canon.**

Large or uncertain ideas should begin in `proposals/` or as a GitHub Issue before being submitted to canon.

## Major early eras

1. **The Founding** — recruitment, preparation, construction of the hidden facility, and conversion of the Peregrine.
2. **Escape from Earth** — launch, pursuit, damage, and the first high-energy propulsion burns.
3. **The Mars Line** — the point beyond which Earth-based military power can no longer realistically stop the Peregrine.
4. **The Asteroid Belt** — the first major resource collection and structural growth.
5. **The Jupiter Assist** — gravity assist, fuel collection, radiation exposure, and preparation for the solar maneuver.
6. **The Sun Dive** — a close solar pass and major propulsion burn to maximize outbound velocity.
7. **The Outer System** — continued growth, resource gathering, and increasing distance from Earth.
8. **The Heliopause** — the symbolic and physical departure from the Sun's domain.
9. **The Long Voyage** — generations live aboard the Peregrine while it crosses interstellar space.
10. **Alpha Centauri** — the ship reaches another stellar system, harvests new matter, expands, and may eventually divide into daughter worldships.

## The central question

The founders leave Earth because they believe concentrated power has become incompatible with human freedom.

Yet life aboard a worldship requires cooperation, shared resources, limits, and obligations to people not yet born.

**How can a civilization preserve individual freedom when its survival depends upon collective responsibility?**

There is no canonical answer.

That is part of the story.
